# Blur & Save privacy policy

Blur & Save processes photos on your device. It reads the photos you open or drop into the app and saves edited copies to the location you choose.

Face detection runs entirely on your device. Blur & Save does not send photos, face detections, or file paths to the developer or to a cloud service. The app has no account, analytics, or advertising.

The files you open and save stay in the locations you choose. You can remove edited copies through File Explorer. If you choose to open an external link in the app, your browser opens that website.

For questions about this policy, use the [Blur & Save issue tracker](https://github.com/traversaro/blur-and-save/issues). Please avoid including private photos or other personal information in a public issue.
