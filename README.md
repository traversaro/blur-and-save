# Blur & Save

Blur faces in photos on Windows. Everything happens on your computer.

1. [Download blur-and-save.exe](https://github.com/traversaro/blur-and-save/releases/latest/download/blur-and-save.exe) and double-click it.
2. Open or drop in a JPG, PNG, or WebP photo. Faces are blurred automatically.
3. Check the preview. Drag over any missed face, then click **Save a copy**.

Your original photo stays untouched. The app works offline.
